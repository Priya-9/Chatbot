# Acme Cloud — Company Overview

Acme Cloud is a cloud-based project management and team collaboration platform for small and
mid-sized businesses. It was founded in 2019 and is headquartered in Austin, Texas, with support teams in
Madrid and Bengaluru.

## Core features

- Task boards (Kanban, list and Gantt timeline views)
- Shared documents and wikis with real-time co-editing
- Time tracking and timesheets
- Client portals so external clients can review and approve work
- Automations: rule-based triggers such as "when a task moves to Done, notify the client"
- Integrations with Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, GitHub, Zapier and QuickBooks
- Native mobile apps for iOS and Android

## Security

Acme Cloud is SOC 2 Type II certified and GDPR compliant. All data is encrypted in transit (TLS 1.3) and at
rest (AES-256). Customer data is hosted on AWS in the United States or the European Union, chosen at sign-up.
Single sign-on (SAML SSO) and audit logs are available on the Business plan.
