# Acme Cloud — Pricing and Plans

All prices are per user per month. Annual billing saves 20%.

## Free plan — $0

- Up to 5 users
- 3 projects
- 1 GB storage per workspace
- Community support

## Pro plan — $12 per user per month ($9.60 billed annually)

- Unlimited projects
- 100 GB storage per workspace
- Gantt timelines, time tracking and automations (1,000 runs per month)
- Client portals
- Email support with a response within 24 hours

## Business plan — $24 per user per month ($19.20 billed annually)

- Everything in Pro
- 1 TB storage per workspace
- Unlimited automation runs
- SAML single sign-on, audit logs and advanced permissions
- Priority 24/7 support with a 1-hour response for urgent issues
- Dedicated customer success manager for teams of 50+ users

## Free trial

Every new workspace gets a 14-day free trial of the Business plan. No credit card is required. At the end
of the trial the workspace moves to the Free plan unless a paid plan is chosen.

## Discounts

- Non-profits and educational institutions: 50% off Pro and Business
- Startups less than 2 years old: 30% off for the first year
