# Acme Cloud — Policies and FAQ

## Refund policy

Monthly plans can be cancelled at any time and remain active until the end of the billing period; monthly
payments are not refunded. Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. After 30 days,
annual plans are refunded pro rata for the unused full months.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

Workspace owners can cancel from Settings → Billing → Cancel plan. Your data stays available on the Free
plan limits, and you can export everything at any time.

## Payment methods

We accept Visa, Mastercard, American Express, PayPal, and bank transfer (annual Business plans only).
Invoices are emailed to the billing contact and available under Settings → Billing → Invoices.

## Can I change plans later?

Yes. Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next billing date.

## Data export and deletion

You can export all projects, tasks and documents as CSV, JSON or PDF from Settings → Data. When a workspace
is deleted, all data is permanently removed from our systems within 30 days, and from backups within 90 days.

## Is there an API?

Yes. Acme Cloud provides a REST API and webhooks on the Pro and Business plans. Rate limit: 600 requests per
minute per workspace. API documentation is at developers.acme-cloud.example.

## Do you offer onboarding?

Business customers with 20+ users get a free onboarding session with a product specialist. Pro customers can
book a paid onboarding package for $299.

## Uptime

Acme Cloud guarantees 99.9% uptime on the Business plan, backed by service credits. Live status is published
at status.acme-cloud.example.
