- We accept cash, UPI, and all debit and credit cards.

## FAQ

- Is root canal painful? It is done under local anaesthesia, so most patients feel little or no pain. It usually takes 1–2 sittings.
- How long do braces take? Usually 12–18 months, with a monthly check-up.
- Do you treat children? Yes, from age 3. Dr. Kulkarni sees kids every day.
- Is the clinic hygienic? We use autoclave sterilisation and single-use disposable kits for every patient.
- Cancellation: please inform us 2 hours before your appointment. There is no cancellation fee.
- Home visits are not available.
