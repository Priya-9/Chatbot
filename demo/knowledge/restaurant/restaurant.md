# The Spice Route Kitchen

A family restaurant in Koramangala, Bengaluru serving North Indian, Mughlai and Coastal cuisine since 2016. Seats 90 guests indoors plus a 30-seat rooftop. Rated 4.4 on Zomato and Google.

## Location and timings

- Address: 80 Feet Road, 4th Block, Koramangala, Bengaluru 560034 (above Croma). Valet parking available.
- Lunch: 12:00 pm – 3:30 pm; dinner: 7:00 pm – 11:30 pm. Open all 7 days.
- The rooftop opens for dinner only and closes during heavy rain.

## Menu highlights and prices

- Starters: Paneer Tikka ₹320, Murgh Malai Tikka ₹380, Mangalorean Prawns Ghee Roast ₹520, Hara Bhara Kebab ₹280
- Mains: Dal Makhani ₹290, Paneer Butter Masala ₹340, Butter Chicken ₹420, Mutton Rogan Josh ₹520, Kerala Fish Curry ₹480
- Biryani: Veg Dum Biryani ₹320, Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani ₹380, Mutton Biryani ₹480
- Breads: Butter Naan ₹70, Garlic Naan ₹90, Malabar Paratha ₹80
- Desserts: Gulab Jamun ₹140, Rasmalai ₹180, Sizzling Brownie ₹240
- Average cost for two: ₹1,400 (without alcohol). Prices exclude 5% GST. No service charge.

## Lunch buffet

- Weekday lunch buffet (Monday to Friday): ₹599 veg, ₹699 non-veg. 4 starters, 6 mains, a live chaat counter and 3 desserts.
- Weekend lunch buffet (Saturday and Sunday): ₹799 veg, ₹899 non-veg. Adds a live tandoor counter and a biryani station.
- Kids aged 5–10 pay half; under 5 eat free.

## Dietary options

- Jain food: most veg dishes can be made without onion, garlic and root vegetables. Please mention it while ordering.
- Vegan and gluten-free options are marked on the menu.
- Separate veg and non-veg kitchens.

## Delivery and takeaway

- Delivery through Swiggy and Zomato within 6 km: Koramangala, HSR Layout, BTM Layout, Indiranagar, Ejipura.
- Direct WhatsApp orders get 10% off for pickup.

## Reservations and events

- Table reservations are recommended on Friday to Sunday evenings. We hold a table for 15 minutes.
- Birthday or anniversary: a free 500 g cake for tables of 6 or more, if mentioned while booking.
- Private dining room for up to 25 guests; set menus from ₹950 per person.
- Outdoor catering for 50–500 guests; share the date and headcount for a quote.
