# IronFit Gym & Yoga Studio

IronFit is a 10,000 sq ft fully air-conditioned gym and yoga studio in Andheri West, Mumbai, open since 2018. Certified trainers (ACE, K11), modern Technogym equipment, a steam room and lockers.

## Location and timings

- Address: 3rd floor, Infiniti Mall Annexe, Link Road, Andheri West, Mumbai 400053. Parking in the mall.
- Monday to Saturday: 5:30 am – 11:00 pm; Sunday: 7:00 am – 1:00 pm
- Ladies-only hours: 11:00 am – 4:00 pm daily, with female trainers. A separate ladies' changing room is always available.
