# BrightPath Academy

BrightPath Academy has coached students for JEE, NEET, Foundation (classes 8–10) and IELTS in Kota and online since 2012. 400+ selections in IITs/NITs and AIIMS/government medical colleges in the last 5 years. Small batches, with a maximum of 40 students.

## Courses and fees

- JEE Main + Advanced, 2-year programme (classes 11–12): ₹1,60,000 per year
- NEET 2-year programme (classes 11–12): ₹1,45,000 per year
- Dropper / repeater batch (JEE or NEET, 1 year): ₹1,50,000
- Foundation (classes 8–10, Maths + Science + Olympiad): ₹55,000 per year
- IELTS (6 weeks, online or offline): ₹12,000; 1-on-1 speaking sessions at ₹800 per hour
- Fees can be paid in 3 instalments. GST at 18% is extra.

## Scholarships

- BrightPath Scholarship Test (BST) every Sunday: up to a 90% fee waiver based on the score.
- 10% sibling discount; 15% discount for wards of defence personnel.

## Batches and timings

- New JEE/NEET batches start on 1 April, 15 May and 1 July. A mid-session batch for droppers starts on 1 October.
- Morning batch 7:00 – 11:00 am (for students in dummy schools); evening batch 4:00 – 8:00 pm (for students in regular schools).
- Foundation: weekdays 5:00 – 7:00 pm, with tests on Saturday.

## Online classes

- All courses are also available as live online classes with recordings, doubt solving on WhatsApp, and weekly tests on our app.
- Online fees are 30% lower than classroom fees.

## Facilities

- Air-conditioned classrooms, a library open 7 am – 10 pm, and daily doubt counters.
- Hostel tie-ups near the campus: ₹12,000 – ₹18,000 per month including meals.
- Monthly parent-teacher meetings; test reports are sent to parents on WhatsApp.

## Demo class and refunds

- A free demo class for 2 days before joining.
- Refunds: 100% within 7 days of joining; 50% within 30 days; no refund after that.
