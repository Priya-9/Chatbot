# Pinewood Retreat, Manali

Pinewood Retreat is a boutique hill resort in Old Manali, Himachal Pradesh, with 24 rooms and cottages among apple orchards, with views of the Beas river. Rated 4.6 on Google and TripAdvisor.

## Rooms and tariff (per night, 2 adults, breakfast included)

- Deluxe room (valley view): ₹4,500 in October–March; ₹6,500 in April–June and Christmas–New Year
- Premium room with balcony: ₹5,500 / ₹8,000
- Family cottage (2 bedrooms, up to 4 adults + 2 kids): ₹9,500 / ₹14,000
- Extra adult: ₹1,200 with mattress and breakfast; children under 6 stay free
- Taxes: 12% GST extra
- Meal plan: add lunch and dinner (MAP) for ₹1,400 per adult per day

## Check-in and policies

- Check-in 12:00 pm, check-out 11:00 am. Early check-in or late check-out depends on availability.
- Booking: a 50% advance confirms the room; the balance is paid at check-in.
- Cancellation: free up to 7 days before arrival; 50% charge within 7 days; no refund within 48 hours or for no-shows.
- Pets are allowed in cottages for ₹1,000 per night.
- A valid photo ID is required for all guests. Unmarried couples are welcome.

## Facilities

- Free Wi-Fi, room heaters, 24-hour hot water, an in-house restaurant (Himachali, North Indian, Continental), a bonfire every evening (₹500 for a private bonfire), free parking.
- Kids' play area and indoor games room.
- Doctor on call.

## Getting here and activities

- 2 km from Manali Mall Road and 3 km from Manali bus stand.
- Pickup from Manali bus stand: ₹500 one way; from Bhuntar airport: ₹3,000.
- We arrange Solang Valley and Atal Tunnel tours, paragliding, river rafting, and Rohtang permits (subject to government rules).
- Snowfall usually happens from late December to February.

## Groups and events

- Destination weddings and corporate offsites for up to 60 guests; the full property can be booked.
