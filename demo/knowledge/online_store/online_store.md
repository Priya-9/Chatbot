# Kavya Ethnic Wear

Kavya Ethnic Wear is a Jaipur-based brand selling handcrafted kurtas, suits, sarees and lehengas online across India since 2019. 50,000+ orders delivered. Hand block prints, pure cotton and chanderi fabrics.

- Hand block print cotton kurta: ₹899 – ₹1,499
- Anarkali kurta with dupatta: ₹2,499 – ₹3,999
- 3-piece cotton suit set (kurta, pant, dupatta): ₹1,799 – ₹2,999
- Chanderi silk saree with blouse piece: ₹3,499 – ₹6,999
- Festive lehenga: ₹7,999 – ₹18,999
- Men's cotton kurta: ₹999 – ₹1,799

## Sizes

- Sizes XS to 3XL (bust 32" to 46"). A size chart is on every product page.
- Custom tailoring is available on suits and lehengas for ₹500 extra; it adds 5 days to dispatch.

## Shipping and delivery

- Free shipping on orders above ₹999; otherwise ₹79.
- Orders are dispatched within 1–2 days from Jaipur.
- Delivery time: metro cities 3–5 days; other cities 4–7 days; within Jaipur, next day.
- International shipping to the USA, UK, Canada, UAE and Australia: 7–12 days, charges shown at checkout.
- A tracking link is sent on WhatsApp and SMS once the order ships.

## Payment and cash on delivery

- UPI, cards, net banking, and card EMI for orders above ₹3,000.
- Cash on delivery (COD) is available across India for orders up to ₹10,000, with a ₹49 COD fee.

## Returns and exchange

- Easy 7-day return or exchange from the delivery date. Items must be unused, with tags.
- Free reverse pickup for exchanges; ₹100 is deducted for returns.
- Refunds go to the original payment method within 5–7 working days. COD refunds go to your UPI ID or bank account.
- Custom-tailored products and final-sale items cannot be returned.
